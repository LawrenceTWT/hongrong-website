01The challenge

Reduce distraction without reducing useful information.

Drivers often switch between maps, traffic cameras, parking apps, and search. That fragmentation adds cognitive load at the exact moment attention should stay on the road.

Our capstone explored how voice interaction and a unified interface could bring the most useful driving information into one Android application.

02The system

Four connected driving tools

/ 4 / ©
01

Smart navigation

Google Maps-based route search and in-app navigation context.

02

GPT voice conversation

Speech-to-text and text-to-speech connect the driver to natural-language help.

03

Parking availability

Government open data helps locate parking facilities and remaining spaces.

04

Live traffic

Traffic camera imagery and road conditions support safer route decisions.

03My contribution

Leading the team while connecting the pieces.

As team lead, I helped define the project plan, coordinate responsibilities, and keep development moving. I also contributed to Android implementation, external API integration, GPT conversation, and the UI/UX flow across the main driving tasks.
