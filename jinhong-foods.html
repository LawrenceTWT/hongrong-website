01The origin

My first real client came through a friend.

His family runs a food-machinery factory. As he prepared to take over the business, he wanted a website that could increase brand exposure and give customers a clearer way to understand the machines.

That changed the responsibility of the work. I was no longer designing for a class brief. The structure, content, and maintenance decisions had to make sense for a real company and a real user.

02BEFORE → GOAL
BEFORE

A real business with technical products, but no clear digital home.

→
GOAL

Make the brand visible and the product catalog understandable.

03RESEARCH / IA

Before the interface

Understand how the industry describes products — and how buyers look for them.

I reviewed other machinery websites, product categories, specifications, and the questions a buyer would need answered.

  1. 01Research peer companies and category patterns
  2. 02Organize product models, specifications, and service content
  3. 03Define clear paths from discovery to inquiry
04DESIGN / PRODUCT DECISIONS

Designed around product decisions

The visual design is only useful if visitors can move from a broad category to the right machine, compare details, and know how to ask for help.

  1. 01

    Mega menu

    Product categories stay visible and easy to scan across a large catalog.

  2. 02

    Product detail pages

    Each machine receives a focused page with imagery, specifications, and models.

  3. 03

    Model comparison

    Visitors can understand the differences between configurations more quickly.

  4. 04

    Inquiry list

    Products can be collected locally before the visitor starts an inquiry.

05The build

The stack supports the business. It is not the story by itself.

I used React and TypeScript to build reusable product patterns, responsive layouts, and clearer maintenance boundaries, then deployed and continued maintaining the site on Cloudflare.

  • 01React + TypeScript
  • 02Reusable, data-driven product presentation
  • 03Responsive layouts and accessible interactions
  • 04Cloudflare deployment and maintenance
