01The origin

My first real client came through a friend.

His family runs a food-machinery factory. As he prepared to take over the business, he wanted a website that could increase brand exposure and give customers a clearer way to understand the machines.

That changed the responsibility of the work. I was no longer designing for a class brief. The structure, content, and maintenance decisions had to make sense for a real company and a real user.

02BEFORE → GOAL

03TECHNOLOGY → OUTCOME

RESEARCH / IA / BUILD

Technology mattered when it made a large catalog easier to use.

The system connects real product content to a clearer path from category to specification and inquiry.

  2. RWD + CSSThe same task on every screenNavigation, product reading, and inquiry remain usable on mobile.
